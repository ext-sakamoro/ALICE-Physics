# The pub PhysicsWorld methods that reach the joints (directly or through the
# methods they call), classified for scripts/joint_entry_points.py:
#   validated-at-entry  its body calls validate_joints / check_step or a method
#                       listed here as validated-at-entry (checked by the scan)
#   n/a                 reaches the joints without solving them by index
# name<TAB>class<TAB>reason
step	validated-at-entry	try_step checks the joints at the start; step panics on StepError::Joint
step_n	validated-at-entry	calls step each frame
try_step	validated-at-entry	check_step runs validate_joints before anything else of the step
step_parallel	validated-at-entry	try_step_parallel checks the joints; step_parallel panics on StepError::Joint
try_step_parallel	validated-at-entry	check_step runs validate_joints before the batched step
step_with_bridge	validated-at-entry	its own check_step at the start; panics on StepError::Joint
substep_with_bridge	validated-at-entry	callable alone, so it runs validate_joints at its start
solve_joints_with_bridge	validated-at-entry	public bridge solve outside a step: validate_joints before uploading
add_joint	n/a	asserts both indices are in range and differ before pushing the joint
remove_joint	n/a	removes by index from the list, solves nothing
remove_body	n/a	drops the joints of the removed body and renumbers the rest, solves nothing
joint_count	n/a	reads the length only
snapshot_world	n/a	serialises the joints as they are, solves nothing
restore_world	n/a	restore_world_checked refuses a joint naming a missing body (DanglingIndex) or a self-joint (SelfJoint) before installing
restore_world_checked	n/a	validate refuses a joint naming a missing body (DanglingIndex) or a self-joint (SelfJoint) before installing
